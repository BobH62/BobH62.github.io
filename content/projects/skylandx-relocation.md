Industry R&D carried out during my internship at
[SkylandX](https://skylandx.com/), a Shenzhen-based spatial-intelligence company
spun out of HKUST research, building 3D laser scanners, software platforms, and
spatial-perception robots (aerial, wheeled, quadruped, and humanoid). The work
targeted **robot indoor relocation in architectural environments** — letting a
mobile robot re-establish its pose inside a built, previously-scanned space.

Re-localization in cluttered, repetitive indoor scenes is a classic SLAM failure
mode: similar-looking corridors, glass façades, and dynamic furniture break
place-recognition, while drift accumulated over long trajectories makes the
"where am I now?" query ambiguous. The project explored how dense 3D perception
and prior building models can be coupled to make relocation robust and fast
enough for autonomous deployment.
