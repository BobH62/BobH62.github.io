A 3-year research series exploiting **active-bending** and elastic gridshells — versatile
structural forms with high form-finding freedom. The work spans design, simulation,
fabrication, and construction:

- **Cable-driven erection of elastic grid formworks** — an industrialized three-stage
  process (prefabrication → cable-coupled erection → post-assembly), validated on scaled
  physical models with 3D-scanned deviation analysis. *(IASS 2022)*
- **Multistable grid-shell formworks for ice/snow structures** — bistable GFRP units
  assembled into programmable 3×3 grids (binary 0/1 states) that shape themselves into
  double-curved formworks for reusable iced-shell construction. **First Winner**,
  *The Arctic Olympic Venues*, Ice & Snow Structure International Invitational Competition
  2022. *(IASS 2022)*
- **Woven Morning Glory** — a full-scale bending-active woven prototype built for the
  Taikoo 2023 exhibition, Canton.

This is where my "digital → physical" thinking started: structural mechanics, FEA, 3D
scanning, and robotic fabrication working together.
