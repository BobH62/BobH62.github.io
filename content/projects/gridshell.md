A 3-year research series exploiting **active-bending** and elastic gridshells —
versatile structural forms with high form-finding freedom. The work spans
design, simulation, fabrication, and construction, across three threads.

## Cable-driven erection of elastic grid formworks

An industrialized three-stage process — **prefabrication → cable-coupled
erection → post-assembly** — that turns a flat grid of elastic strips into a
double-curved shell by pulling cables, then stiffens it and casts concrete
against it. Scaled physical models were built and **3D-scanned** to measure
deviation against the FEA simulation.

## Multistable grid-shell formworks for ice/snow structures

Bistable GFRP units snap between two stable states (0/1); assembled into a 3×3
grid they become a **programmable surface** whose binary configuration sculpts
the double-curved formwork. The same formwork is reused across many shapes, then
used to cast reusable iced shells. **First Winner**, *The Arctic Olympic
Venues*, Ice & Snow Structure International Invitational Competition 2022.

## Woven Morning Glory

A full-scale bending-active woven prototype built for the Taikoo 2023
exhibition in Canton. Mesh models drive the weaving of timber laths into three
funnel forms balanced in a bending-active system — built without complex 3D
positioning, an accessible alternative to conventional doubly-curved
construction.
