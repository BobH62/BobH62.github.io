**Solar Decathlon Middle East 2021** — a real, student-led project from design to construction. The house was produced in a Dongguan factory and shipped to Dubai for assembly and competition during Expo 2020. We won **Overall Champion**.

My contributions:

- **Smart-home system** — built on **Home Assistant**, integrating appliances over **Zigbee / MQTT / Modbus**; automated scenarios (blinds, skylights, climate) via YAML; 24/7 remote monitoring and control through a Raspberry Pi + Zerotier VPN.
- **Robotic fabrication of furniture** — designed and robot-milled molds for unconventional GRC concrete furniture (a tea table and washstand inspired by Chinese landscapes), produced in a GRC factory and shipped overseas.

A rare chance to take a building from a digital model to a lived-in, instrumented house on another continent.

- **Role**: Smart-home system & robotic furniture fabrication
- **Stack**: Home Assistant, Python, Raspberry Pi, Zigbee/MQTT/Modbus, robotic milling
