## Solar Decathlon Middle East 2021 — Overall Champion

A student-led full-size solar house, designed and built in Dongguan and shipped to Dubai for Expo 2020. I built its smart-home system (Home Assistant over Zigbee/MQTT/Modbus, Raspberry Pi + Zerotier VPN) and robotically fabricated its furniture. Our team won **Overall Champion**.

## Ice & Snow Structure International Invitational Competition 2022 — First Winner

*The Arctic Olympic Venues* — sustainable Arctic Olympic venues built with **multistable grid-shell formworks** for iced shells. Reusable, programmable bistable GFRP grids shape double-curved ice structures with minimal material. **First Winner.**

## From Architecture to Robotics to AGI

I started in **architecture** (B.Arch, SCUT) — digital design, robotic fabrication, and full-scale construction. That "design-to-physical" mindset carried into a **robotics MPhil** (HKUST): cross-modal LiDAR–BIM registration, SLAM, and 3D perception. Today I work on **world models for autonomous driving** at Zhuoyu (formerly DJI Automotive). I care about how ideas migrate across architecture, robotics, and AI.
