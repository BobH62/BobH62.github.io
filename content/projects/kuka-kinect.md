Communicating with different robots is not always convenient for designers.
**Kuka and Kinect** uses a **Kinect** sensor for **gesture control** of a Kuka
industrial arm, connected over **socket communication** — letting a designer
direct the robot by hand motion instead of hand-coding every move.

The project explores what kinds of opportunities open up when the robot becomes
gesture-driven in a design studio: rapid physical sketching, hands-on toolpath
demonstration, and a more intuitive interface between human intent and robotic
execution.
